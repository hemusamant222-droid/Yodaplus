## 24–32s · 03 / THE GOVERNANCE GAP
Who checks? Who owns it?
When ownership is unclear, an AI error can become an accountability failure.
EVIDENCE → Can each material claim be traced?
AUTHORITY → Who can approve or stop its use?

## 32–40s · 04 / THE CONTROL LAYER
Verify before it moves capital.
Match controls to the use case and the consequence of an error.
GROUND + CHECK → Approved sources; verify claims + dates
GATE + REVIEW → Escalate uncertainty; human approval
LOG + MONITOR → Record evidence; test and track failures
Retrieval reduces risk. It does not eliminate it.
