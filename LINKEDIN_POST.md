3 things to know: Why the trading desk is the hardest frontier for AI.

A trading desk puts AI under three pressures at once:

1. Speed: A useful signal can lose its edge before the order reaches the market. Latency, slippage and execution costs matter alongside accuracy.

2. Risk: A confident prediction is not a risk limit. Position controls, audit trails and human oversight need to be part of the workflow.

3. Adaptation: Markets change. Liquidity shifts and familiar patterns can break. Strong backtests are a starting point, not a guarantee of live performance.

The opportunity goes beyond a better model: build a workflow that is fast enough to act, controlled enough to trust and adaptive enough to last.
